# Going back

- **CMF:** delete `Mods\mod0000_ConjunctionMapFramework` and install CMF 1.12.0 from its release, with its bin menu file. The controller changes (crosshair, R3, Quest List stick and A) are gone, and so are the Player setting level and the size multipliers. 1.12.1 was never released, so 1.12.0 is the version to go back to.
- **CMF Customizer:** delete `Mods\modCMFCustomizer` and `bin\config\r4game\user_config_matrix\pc\modCMFCustomizer.xml`. Remove it before going back to a CMF older than 1.12.2; with an older CMF it sends nothing, so nothing breaks, but its page is useless.
- **In-game menu add-on:** go back to 1.10.0 from the 1.10.0 release. The Status rows then show their value in the right-hand column again, which Ink and Iron's menu does not draw.
- **Map Quest Insight adapter:** go back to 2.5.4. The Quest List then acts as before on a controller (a D-pad press may move twice, and the stick moves the list).

Nothing here writes into saves.
