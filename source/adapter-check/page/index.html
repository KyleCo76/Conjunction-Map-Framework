Check your map mod list

Find CMF adapter guidance from names in your Mods folder list, mods.settings, Vortex list or Mod Organizer 2 list. Paste text below; results update as you type.

In Windows, open a terminal in the game's Mods folder and run cmd /c dir /b /ad. Copy the folder names here. You can also paste quoted paths or your mod manager's display names.

Results are names found in pasted text, not proof that mods or adapters are enabled or loading. Disabled lines (including Enabled=0 and MO2's minus prefix) still count. This page cannot read installed versions or your game. Unknown names are not assessed.
